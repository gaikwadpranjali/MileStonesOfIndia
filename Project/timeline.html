Journey Through Time

A chronological exploration of 5,000 years of Indian history

3300 BCE

Early Indus Valley settlements begin in the Indian subcontinent, featuring advanced urban planning and drainage systems.

Explore Ancient
1500 BCE

The Vedic Period begins with the composition of the Vedas, laying the philosophical foundations of Hinduism and Sanskrit.

Learn More
322 BCE

Chandragupta Maurya establishes the Mauryan Empire. Later, Ashoka spreads Buddhism and the principles of non-violence.

View Era
320 CE

The Gupta Golden Age begins, bringing major advances in science, mathematics (concept of zero), astronomy, and classical arts.

Discover
1206 CE

The Delhi Sultanate is established, leading to a unique synthesis of Indo-Islamic architecture, culture, and administration.

Explore Medieval
1526 CE

Babur establishes the Mughal Empire after the First Battle of Panipat, eventually building iconic wonders like the Taj Mahal.

View Details
1757 CE

The Battle of Plassey marks the beginning of British political control and the expansion of the East India Company.

Learn History
1857 CE

The First War of Independence (Sepoy Mutiny), a massive uprising against the British East India Company's rule.

Read More
1930 CE

Mahatma Gandhi leads the historic Salt March (Dandi March), galvanizing the nationwide Civil Disobedience Movement.

Freedom Struggle
1947 CE

India gains independence from British rule on August 15th, alongside the tragic partition that created Pakistan.

Independence
1950 CE

India becomes a sovereign, democratic republic and adopts its Constitution, the longest written national constitution in the world.

Modern India
1991 CE

Economic Liberalization: India opens its markets to global trade, sparking decades of rapid economic and technological growth.

Economic Boom
2023 CE

Chandrayaan-3 successfully lands near the lunar south pole, making India the first nation to achieve this historic milestone.

Space Age
2024 CE

Aditya-L1, India's first solar mission, successfully reaches the Sun's Lagrange point, marking a giant leap in space-based scientific research and observation.

Solar Mission
2026 CE

India cements its position as the world's 3rd largest economy, leading global initiatives in green hydrogen, artificial intelligence, and Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI).

Future Forward
