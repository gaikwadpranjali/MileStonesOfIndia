Modern India

Building a democratic republic, economic powerhouse, and global technological leader

A New Republic Rises

Following independence in 1947, India embarked on the monumental task of unifying a diverse subcontinent into a modern, democratic republic. The adoption of the Constitution in 1950 laid the foundation for the world's largest democracy, ensuring justice, liberty, and equality for over a billion citizens.

Over the decades, India has transformed from an agrarian economy into a global powerhouse. Through the Green Revolution, economic liberalization, and a booming IT sector, the nation has lifted hundreds of millions out of poverty. Today, India stands at the forefront of space exploration, digital innovation, and global geopolitics.

Key Milestones

1950 CE
Adoption of the Constitution

India officially becomes a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic, adopting the longest written national constitution in the world, guaranteeing fundamental rights to all citizens.

1960s–1970s
Green Revolution & ISRO

Transformative agricultural advancements ensure food security for the growing population. Simultaneously, the establishment of ISRO lays the foundation for India's indigenous space program.

1991 CE
Economic Liberalization

Under PM P.V. Narasimha Rao and Finance Minister Dr. Manmohan Singh, India opens its economy to global markets, dismantling the License Raj and sparking decades of unprecedented GDP growth.

Explore →
1998 CE
Pokhran-II Nuclear Tests

India successfully conducts underground nuclear tests in the Pokhran desert, declaring itself a full-fledged nuclear weapons state and asserting its strategic autonomy on the global stage.

2000s–2010s
The IT & Digital Boom

India emerges as the "back office of the world" and later pioneers world-class digital public infrastructure like UPI and Aadhaar, transforming the global tech landscape and financial inclusion.

2023 CE
Chandrayaan-3 Moon Landing

ISRO makes history by successfully landing a spacecraft near the lunar south pole, making India the first country in the world to achieve this monumental feat in space exploration.
