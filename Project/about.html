About the Project

Preserving the past, inspiring the future

Our Mission

To make 5,000 years of Indian history accessible, engaging, and inspiring for everyone.

India's history is not just a series of dates and events; it is a living, breathing tapestry of cultures, innovations, struggles, and triumphs. "Milestones of India" was created to bridge the gap between academic history and everyday curiosity.

Whether you are a student, a history enthusiast, or someone looking to reconnect with their roots, this platform provides a curated, visually rich journey through the eras that shaped the world's largest democracy.
